## What this assessment tests
This assessment evaluates a candidate's ability to plan and execute a Google Ads campaign for a specific service, demonstrating their practical knowledge of paid advertising and ability to think through campaign logic. A strong hire for this role must show evidence of hands-on campaign experience, understanding of search intent, and ability to explain and defend their decisions. They should also demonstrate performance marketing thinking and ability to structure and manage a campaign effectively.

## What each category means here
* **Problem understanding**: In this assessment, problem understanding refers to the candidate's ability to comprehend the specific requirements of the AI Training for Legal Teams service and tailor their campaign planning and execution accordingly, taking into account the target audience, geographic targeting, and other constraints.
* **Depth of reasoning**: Depth of reasoning in this context involves the candidate's ability to analyze the campaign's objectives, targeting, and bidding strategy, and provide a clear and well-supported explanation of their decisions, including trade-offs and alternatives considered.
* **Domain craft**: Domain craft in this assessment refers to the candidate's demonstration of specific skills in Google Ads campaign planning and execution, including keyword research, ad copywriting, campaign settings, and performance metrics analysis, at a level that shows expertise in paid advertising.
* **Practical execution**: Practical execution in this assessment means the candidate's ability to provide a concrete and actionable campaign plan, including specific keywords, ad copy, targeting settings, and budget allocation, that demonstrates their ability to manage and optimize a real campaign.
* **Communication**: Communication in this context involves the candidate's ability to clearly and concisely explain their campaign logic and decision-making, both in writing and in the video walkthrough, using structured and easy-to-follow language.

## What a strong answer does
* Provides a clear and well-structured campaign planning document that addresses all required elements, including keyword strategy, ad creation, and campaign settings.
* Demonstrates a thorough understanding of search intent and how it influences keyword selection and ad copywriting.
* Shows evidence of performance marketing thinking, including clear objectives, targeting, and bidding strategy.
* Explains and defends campaign decisions, including trade-offs and alternatives considered.
* Provides specific examples and metrics from previous campaign experience, including campaign structure, targeting strategy, budget management, and optimization decisions.
* Clearly discloses level of involvement in previous campaigns and provides context for any confidential or sensitive information.
* Demonstrates ability to measure success and monitor key metrics after campaign launch.

## What a weak answer does
* Fails to provide a clear and well-structured campaign planning document, omitting key elements or providing vague or generic information.
* Demonstrates a lack of understanding of search intent and its influence on keyword selection and ad copywriting.
* Shows limited or no evidence of performance marketing thinking, including unclear objectives, targeting, or bidding strategy.
* Fails to explain and defend campaign decisions, including trade-offs and alternatives considered.
* Provides generic or theoretical information without specific examples or metrics from previous campaign experience.
* Fails to disclose level of involvement in previous campaigns or provides unclear or misleading information.
