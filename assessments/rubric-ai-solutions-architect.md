## What this assessment tests
This assessment evaluates a candidate's ability to structure a technical AI delivery program, translate a solution into a realistic execution plan, and demonstrate technical credibility as an AI Solutions Architect. A strong hire for this role must demonstrate the ability to manage dependencies and risks, communicate credibly with both technical and non-technical audiences, and build a lightweight prototype that supports delivery operations. The assessment tests the candidate's ability to apply their skills and knowledge in a practical and realistic scenario.

## What each category means here
* **Problem understanding**: In this assessment, problem understanding refers to the candidate's ability to comprehend the specific requirements and constraints of the Patient Acquisition & Growth Agent implementation, and to demonstrate an understanding of the hospital client's needs and goals.
* **Depth of reasoning**: Depth of reasoning in this assessment involves the candidate's ability to analyze complex technical and operational issues, identify key dependencies and risks, and develop well-supported conclusions and recommendations.
* **Domain craft**: Domain craft in this assessment refers to the candidate's demonstration of specific skills and knowledge relevant to the role of an AI Solutions Architect, such as the ability to design and implement AI solutions, integrate with existing systems, and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.
* **Practical execution**: Practical execution in this assessment involves the candidate's ability to develop a concrete and actionable delivery plan, including specific milestones, timelines, and resource allocations, as well as a functional prototype that demonstrates their technical skills.
* **Communication**: Communication in this assessment refers to the candidate's ability to clearly and concisely articulate their thoughts and ideas, both in writing and in the video walkthrough, and to effectively communicate with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

## What a strong answer does
* Provides a clear and concise delivery plan that addresses the specific requirements and constraints of the implementation
* Identifies and mitigates key dependencies and risks, and develops a comprehensive risk management plan
* Demonstrates technical credibility through the development of a functional prototype and a clear understanding of AI tools and technologies
* Communicates effectively with both technical and non-technical stakeholders, and provides regular progress updates and status reports
* Prioritizes tasks and allocates resources effectively, and demonstrates an ability to work under time constraints
* Uses AI tools and technologies effectively to accelerate delivery and improve outcomes
* Develops a comprehensive and realistic milestone plan, including specific timelines and resource allocations

## What a weak answer does
* Fails to provide a clear and concise delivery plan, or neglects to address key dependencies and risks
* Demonstrates a lack of technical credibility, or fails to provide a functional prototype
* Communicates poorly with stakeholders, or fails to provide regular progress updates and status reports
* Fails to prioritize tasks and allocate resources effectively, or demonstrates an inability to work under time constraints
* Fails to use AI tools and technologies effectively, or neglects to consider their potential benefits and limitations
* Provides an unrealistic or overly ambitious milestone plan, or fails to allocate sufficient resources to key tasks and activities

## Score bands
* 90-100: The candidate provides a comprehensive and realistic delivery plan, demonstrates technical credibility, and communicates effectively with stakeholders. The prototype is functional and well-designed, and the candidate prioritizes tasks and allocates resources effectively.
* 75-89: The candidate provides a good delivery plan, demonstrates some technical credibility, and communicates effectively with stakeholders. The prototype is functional, but may have some limitations or areas for improvement.
* 60-74: The candidate provides a fair delivery plan, demonstrates some technical skills, and communicates adequately with stakeholders. The prototype may have some significant limitations or flaws.
* 40-59: The candidate provides a poor delivery plan, demonstrates limited technical credibility, and communicates poorly with stakeholders. The prototype may be non-functional or poorly designed.
* 0-39: The candidate fails to provide a delivery plan, demonstrates no technical credibility, and communicates very poorly with stakeholders. The prototype is non-functional or absent.
