## What this assessment tests
This assessment evaluates a candidate's ability to design and plan a document pipeline automation program, demonstrating their understanding of the scope, stakeholder management, and technical requirements. A strong hire for this role must show expertise in program management, communication, and AI workflow integration. They should be able to balance technical and business aspects, ensuring a feasible and executable plan.

## What each category means here
* **Problem understanding**: The candidate demonstrates a clear grasp of the automation program's objectives, constraints, and stakeholder expectations, addressing the specific challenges of the scenario.
* **Depth of reasoning**: The candidate provides a thorough analysis of the program's components, such as scope decomposition, risk management, and AI workflow integration, weighing trade-offs and alternatives.
* **Domain craft**: The candidate showcases expertise in program management, particularly in document pipeline automation, demonstrating a deep understanding of workflow automation, risk mitigation, and communication planning.
* **Practical execution**: The candidate provides a concrete, actionable plan with specific steps, timelines, and resource allocations, ensuring the program's execution readiness.
* **Communication**: The candidate presents a clear, concise, and well-structured submission, effectively conveying their plan and assumptions to stakeholders.

## What a strong answer does
* Decomposes the scope into manageable workstreams with clear milestones and dependencies.
* Develops a detailed meeting cadence map with agenda templates and decision rights for internal and client-facing meetings.
* Establishes a comprehensive communication matrix for leadership, delivery teams, and client stakeholders.
* Identifies and mitigates potential risks, including those related to AI workflow integration and dependency management.
* Provides a realistic first 30-day execution plan with staffing assumptions and readiness checks.
* Includes a well-structured AI workflow with validation checks for timeline feasibility and dependency realism.
* Offers concrete examples of AI suggestions corrected due to domain constraints.

## What a weak answer does
* Fails to adequately decompose the scope, resulting in unclear workstreams and milestones.
* Lacks a clear meeting cadence map, leading to potential communication breakdowns.
* Omits key stakeholders from the communication matrix, risking inadequate information sharing.
* Does not properly identify and mitigate risks, potentially leading to program delays or failures.
* Provides an unrealistic or incomplete execution plan, neglecting essential staffing assumptions and readiness checks.
* Fails to demonstrate a clear understanding of AI workflow integration and its limitations.

## Score bands
* 90-100: The submission demonstrates exceptional understanding, depth, and practicality, providing a comprehensive and well-structured plan that exceeds expectations.
* 75-89: The submission shows strong understanding and reasoning, with a well-structured plan that meets most requirements, but may lack some detail or clarity.
* 60-74: The submission demonstrates a good understanding of the problem, but may lack depth in analysis or practicality in execution, with some areas requiring improvement.
* 40-59: The submission shows a limited understanding of the problem, with significant gaps in analysis, planning, or communication, requiring substantial revision.
* 0-39: The submission fails to demonstrate a basic understanding of the problem, with severe deficiencies in analysis, planning, and communication, requiring a complete rework.
