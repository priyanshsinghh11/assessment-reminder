## What this assessment tests  
A strong hire must demonstrate the ability to **turn a high‑value, long‑form report into a publish‑ready, channel‑native content package** within a tight 2‑hour window.  The candidate needs to show rapid editorial judgment, production‑level design and video skills, AI‑augmented workflow fluency, and clear KPI thinking that ties each asset to business outcomes.

---

## What each category means here  

- **Problem understanding** – The candidate recognises the exact deliverables (short‑form video, LinkedIn post + visual, two X posts + visual, KPI plan, walkthrough) and works within the 2‑hour limit. Markers look for evidence that the candidate scoped the work, chose angles that match the report’s “AI‑value‑roadmap” theme, and respected the required formats and constraints.  

- **Depth of reasoning** – The answer explains *why* each angle, hook, visual, and KPI was chosen, compares alternatives (e.g., carousel vs single graphic, thread vs two standalone X posts), and ties decisions to the target audience (enterprise leaders who have failed pilots). Trade‑offs such as time saved vs creative polish should be discussed.  

- **Domain craft** – This role is a **content‑production / growth‑marketing** specialist. Markers look for:  
  - Video editing that meets the hook‑in‑first‑3‑seconds, tight pacing, caption accuracy, and visual hierarchy.  
  - Copy that uses an operator‑minded, data‑driven voice and follows LinkedIn/X best‑practice length, tone, and formatting.  
  - Graphic design that respects platform dimensions, typography, colour contrast, and avoids placeholder text.  
  - AI‑tool usage (e.g., generative text, image‑to‑text, auto‑captioning, motion‑graphics templates) that demonstrably speeds the workflow.  

- **Practical execution** – All assets are **production‑ready**: the video file can be uploaded directly, the graphics are correctly sized and exported, the copy is ready to post, and the KPI sheet contains concrete numbers and decision thresholds. The walkthrough video clearly walks the reviewer through the process.  

- **Communication** – The submission is organised, labelled, and easy to scan. The written explanations (edit bullets, KPI rationale, walkthrough script) are concise, logically ordered, and free of jargon that obscures meaning.

---

## What a strong answer does  

- Provides **five edit‑decision bullets** that name the exact hook used, the specific cuts removed (e.g., “trimmed the 12‑second intro to a 2‑second hook”), the on‑screen text hierarchy (large headline, secondary bullet, CTA), the CTA wording, and one concrete test for a second version.  
- Delivers a **30‑60 s video** that starts with a compelling visual or question in the first 3 seconds, has clean cuts, synced captions, and on‑screen text that follows a clear visual hierarchy; the file is exported in MP4, 1080p, < 15 MB.  
- Supplies a **LinkedIn post** (≤ 1,300 characters) with a **single‑slide or ≤ 5‑slide carousel** that uses the brand font, correct 1,200 × 627 px dimensions, no lorem‑ipsum, and includes a data‑point or quote from the report.  
- Provides **two X assets**: one standalone tweet (≤ 280 chars) and one thread (≥ 2 tweets) *or* two standalone tweets, with a brief rationale for the chosen structure; at least one tweet includes a **native‑size graphic** (e.g., 1,200 × 675 px) that visualises a key metric from the report.  
- Submits a **KPI plan** (≤ 1 page) that lists for each channel: primary KPI (e.g., video completion rate, LinkedIn post click‑through rate, X engagement rate), why it matters, a numeric success threshold (e.g., ≥ 45 % completion), and a clear corrective action if under‑performed.  
- Includes a **≤ 3‑minute walkthrough video** that walks through angle selection, cross‑channel adaptation, edit workflow, AI tools used (e.g., Descript for auto‑captioning, Midjourney for graphics, ChatGPT for copy), and points where human judgment overrode AI.  
- Shows **AI‑native workflow**: timestamps or screen‑captures in the walkthrough that prove AI tools saved ≥ 30 % of the time (e.g., “auto‑generated captions in 12 seconds vs manual 2 min”).  
- Organises everything in a **single Google‑Drive folder** with clearly named files (e.g., `Video_ShortForm.mp4`, `LinkedIn_Post.docx`, `X_Post1.png`, `KPI_Plan.pdf`, `Walkthrough.mp4`).  

---

## What a weak answer does  

- Omits one or more required deliverables (e.g., no KPI sheet, missing X visual, no edit‑decision bullets).  
- Provides a video that exceeds 60 seconds, lacks a hook in the first 3 seconds, or is missing captions/on‑screen text hierarchy.  
- Supplies copy that is generic, exceeds platform length limits, or uses placeholder text in graphics.  
- KPI section lists only “likes” or “views” without numeric targets or decision logic.  
- Walkthrough video is > 3 minutes, silent, or only shows the screen without narration of reasoning.  
- AI tools are mentioned but no evidence of actual usage or time‑saving impact is shown.  
- Files are scattered across multiple links, poorly named, or the Google‑Drive folder is incomplete, causing the reviewer to miss assets.
