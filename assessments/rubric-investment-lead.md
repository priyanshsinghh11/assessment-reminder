## What this assessment tests
A strong hire for the AI-Native Strategic Investment Lead role must demonstrate the ability to conduct thorough investment analyses, identify key risks and opportunities, and communicate complex ideas effectively. They must also show a deep understanding of the education technology industry and the ability to leverage AI tools to support their work. Ultimately, they must be able to make informed investment recommendations that align with Ajaia's portfolio and strategy.

## What each category means here
* **Problem understanding**: The candidate demonstrates a clear grasp of the investment opportunity, including the company's financials, market position, and competitive landscape, as well as Ajaia's existing portfolio and strategic goals.
* **Depth of reasoning**: The candidate provides thoughtful and well-supported analysis of the investment opportunity, including identification of key risks and opportunities, and evaluation of the company's potential for growth and returns.
* **Domain craft**: The candidate demonstrates expertise in education technology and venture capital investing, including knowledge of industry trends, market dynamics, and key metrics such as revenue growth, customer acquisition, and retention.
* **Practical execution**: The candidate provides concrete and actionable recommendations for investment, including specific diligence priorities, deal structure, and value creation plans.
* **Communication**: The candidate communicates complex ideas clearly and concisely, both in writing and in the video submission, and demonstrates the ability to distill key insights and recommendations into a clear and compelling narrative.

## What a strong answer does
* Provides a clear and well-supported calculation of the company's implied post-money valuation and revenue multiple
* Identifies three strong reasons to invest in the company, with specific metrics and evidence to support each reason
* Articulates five significant risks to the investment, with clear explanations of why each risk matters and what evidence would mitigate or exacerbate it
* Prioritizes three key diligence areas, with specific questions to answer and clear explanations of how the answers would inform the investment decision
* Presents a clear and well-supported investment recommendation, including a decision to invest or not, and specific conditions or structure for the deal
* Provides a concise and actionable 12-month value creation plan, with specific actions, owners, and metrics to move

## What a weak answer does
* Fails to provide clear and well-supported calculations of key metrics, such as implied post-money valuation and revenue multiple
* Identifies reasons to invest that are not supported by specific evidence or metrics
* Fails to articulate significant risks to the investment, or provides only superficial analysis of potential risks
* Prioritizes diligence areas that are not relevant to the investment decision, or fails to provide clear explanations of how the answers would inform the decision
* Presents an investment recommendation that is not well-supported by analysis or evidence
* Provides a vague or unrealistic 12-month value creation plan, with unclear actions, owners, or metrics to move

## Score bands
* 90-100: The candidate provides a comprehensive and well-supported investment analysis, with clear and compelling recommendations and a strong understanding of the education technology industry and venture capital investing.
* 75-89: The candidate provides a good investment analysis, with some areas of strength and weakness, and demonstrates a solid understanding of the industry and investing principles.
* 60-74: The candidate provides a fair investment analysis, with some significant areas of weakness, and demonstrates a limited understanding of the industry and investing principles.
* 40-59: The candidate provides a poor investment analysis, with significant areas of weakness and a limited understanding of the industry and investing principles.
* 0-39: The candidate fails to provide a credible investment analysis, with significant gaps in understanding and analysis, and demonstrates a lack of familiarity with the industry and investing principles.
