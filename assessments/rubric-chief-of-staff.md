## What this assessment tests
This assessment evaluates a candidate's ability to operate effectively as a Chief of Staff in a fast-moving, AI-native organization, specifically their capacity to prioritize under ambiguity, support executive decision-making, build scalable operating systems, drive cross-functional execution, and leverage AI as a tool. A strong hire for this role must demonstrate strategic judgment, prioritization skills, and the ability to create visibility, accountability, and decision-making leverage across the organization. They must also be able to communicate effectively with executives and other stakeholders.

## What each category means here
* **Problem understanding**: In this assessment, problem understanding refers to the candidate's ability to grasp the complexities of the scenario, identify the key challenges and constraints, and develop solutions that address the specific needs of the organization.
* **Depth of reasoning**: Depth of reasoning in this context involves the candidate's ability to analyze complex situations, weigh trade-offs, and develop well-supported conclusions that demonstrate a deep understanding of the organization's goals and priorities.
* **Domain craft**: Domain craft in this assessment refers to the candidate's mastery of the specific skills required to be a successful Chief of Staff, including strategic planning, prioritization, executive communication, systems thinking, crisis leadership, and AI-native execution.
* **Practical execution**: Practical execution in this context involves the candidate's ability to develop concrete, actionable plans and solutions that can be implemented in a real-world setting, including the ability to identify key metrics, develop effective dashboards, and create clear and concise updates for executives.
* **Communication**: Communication in this assessment refers to the candidate's ability to clearly and concisely explain their thoughts, plans, and decisions, both in writing and in a video submission, and to effectively communicate with executives and other stakeholders.

## What a strong answer does
* Provides a clear and concise executive summary that effectively communicates the key points and recommendations
* Develops a comprehensive executive operating dashboard that includes key metrics, meeting cadence, accountability mechanisms, escalation indicators, and areas requiring CEO visibility
* Prioritizes initiatives based on a clear and well-supported rationale, and explains the trade-offs and decisions made
* Demonstrates a deep understanding of the organization's goals and priorities, and develops solutions that align with these goals
* Effectively communicates the role of AI in supporting workflow and decision-making, and explains how AI tools were used to accelerate work and support key decisions
* Provides a clear and concise video submission that effectively explains the candidate's thought process, decisions, and recommendations
* Develops a comprehensive crisis leadership plan that includes immediate priorities, delegation and ownership, communication, escalation, and recovery plans

## What a weak answer does
* Fails to provide a clear and concise executive summary, or does not effectively communicate the key points and recommendations
* Develops an incomplete or ineffective executive operating dashboard that lacks key metrics, meeting cadence, accountability mechanisms, escalation indicators, or areas requiring CEO visibility
* Prioritizes initiatives based on incomplete or unsupported analysis, or fails to explain the trade-offs and decisions made
* Demonstrates a lack of understanding of the organization's goals and priorities, or develops solutions that do not align with these goals
* Fails to effectively communicate the role of AI in supporting workflow and decision-making, or does not explain how AI tools were used to accelerate work and support key decisions
* Provides a video submission that is unclear, incomplete, or ineffective in explaining the candidate's thought process, decisions, and recommendations

## Score bands
* 90-100: The submission demonstrates exceptional understanding, analysis, and communication skills, and provides comprehensive and well-supported solutions that effectively address the challenges and priorities of the organization.
* 75-89: The submission demonstrates strong understanding, analysis, and communication skills, and provides effective solutions that address the challenges and priorities of the organization, but may lack some depth or clarity in certain areas.
* 60-74: The submission demonstrates adequate understanding, analysis, and communication skills, and provides some effective solutions, but may lack clarity, depth, or completeness in certain areas, or may not fully address the challenges and priorities of the organization.
* 40-59: The submission demonstrates limited understanding, analysis, and communication skills, and provides incomplete or ineffective solutions that do not fully address the challenges and priorities of the organization.
* 0-39: The submission demonstrates a lack of understanding, analysis, and communication skills, and provides no effective solutions or fails to address the challenges and priorities of the organization.
