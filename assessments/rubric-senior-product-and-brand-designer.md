## What this assessment tests  
A strong hire must be able to translate sophisticated AI concepts into instantly‑graspable, platform‑native creative assets while orchestrating the whole production pipeline with AI tools.  They also need to demonstrate strategic thinking about brand positioning, audience‑specific storytelling, and measurable rollout plans.

## What each category means here  

- **Problem understanding** – The candidate shows they grasp the brief: a premium visual language for Ajaia’s shift to “AI Implementation & Partnership,” native Instagram & LinkedIn formats, and a full‑cycle workflow (design → video → calendar → documentation).  
- **Depth of reasoning** – The answer explains *why* each design, copy, or sequencing choice was made (e.g., why a Reel leads the week, why a carousel suits LinkedIn), cites trade‑offs between AI‑generated vs. hand‑crafted elements, and connects KPI choices to business goals.  
- **Domain craft** – Demonstrates concrete creative‑director, social‑media‑manager, and AI‑power‑user skills: premium typography, grid systems, data‑visualization best practices, AI‑image/video generation (Midjourney, Runway, etc.), AI voiceover (ElevenLabs), caption automation, and platform‑specific specs (aspect ratios, caption length).  
- **Practical execution** – Supplies ready‑to‑publish assets (Figma/Canva template, PNG/JPG graphics, MP4 Reel), a complete 7‑day calendar with hook, asset type, and KPI, and a WORKFLOW.md that lists every tool, prompt, and manual fix.  
- **Communication** – The submission is clearly organized (folder structure, labeled files), uses concise, punchy copy free of “AI‑isms,” and the video walkthrough is well‑structured (thought process → workflow demo → hook rationale).

## What a strong answer does  

- Provides **one master post template** (Figma or Canva) plus **three concrete screen mock‑ups** that follow the same grid, typography, and AI‑generated visual style.  
- Delivers a **single infographic** that visualizes the “AI hype vs. enterprise reality” gap, using at least one AI‑generated image and hand‑crafted typography/layout.  
- Submits a **30‑45 s Reel** that includes: AI‑generated voiceover, dynamic captions, and a blend of stock + AI‑generated B‑roll; the final MP4 meets Instagram specs.  
- Includes a **7‑day launch calendar** (Markdown table) with date, platform, hook, asset type, primary KPI, and a brief paragraph justifying the sequencing logic.  
- Supplies a **WORKFLOW.md** that lists every AI tool, shows 2‑3 exact prompts (with parameters), and explains the “human edge” fixes for each AI‑generated asset.  
- Uses **copy that is punchy, professional, and free of filler words** like “delve,” “tapestry,” or “embark.”  
- Provides a **3‑5 min Loom/YouTube walkthrough** that is logically segmented (design rationale → AI workflow demo → video hook justification).  
- All files are **properly named and organized** in a single Google‑Drive link, with working Figma/Canva links and a functional video URL.

## What a weak answer does  

- Submits a generic template that could belong to any tech brand, with no clear visual “north star” or AI‑generated elements.  
- Omits the required three‑screen examples, or provides only a low‑resolution PNG without the source file.  
- Leaves out the infographic, or creates one that is text‑heavy, un‑styled, and lacks any AI‑generated asset.  
- Provides a Reel that is >1 minute, lacks AI voiceover or captions, or is delivered as a raw project file instead of a final MP4.  
- Misses the WORKFLOW.md, or lists tools without prompts or without a “human edge” section.  
- Uses filler “AI‑isms” in copy, or writes copy that feels more like a press release than a platform‑native hook.  
- Supplies a calendar that is just a list of dates with no KPI, no hook rationale, and no platform differentiation.  
- Files are scattered across multiple links, poorly labeled, or the video walkthrough is missing or exceeds the time limit.

## Score bands  

- **90‑100** – All five categories excel; deliverables are complete, premium, and instantly publishable; reasoning is thorough; workflow is transparent; communication is crystal‑clear.  
- **75‑89** – Minor gaps (e.g., one deliverable slightly off spec, or a small lapse in copy polish) but overall strong execution and clear strategic rationale.  
- **60‑74** – Most deliverables present, but at least two categories show noticeable weakness (e.g., missing workflow detail, generic design, or weak KPI justification).  
- **40‑59** – Several required pieces are absent or low‑quality; reasoning is shallow; copy contains multiple AI‑isms; overall submission feels unfinished.  
- **0‑39** – Major portions of the brief are missing (no template, no Reel, no workflow); little to no evidence of AI tool mastery; communication is chaotic or unreadable.
