## What this assessment tests
This assessment evaluates a candidate's ability to deliver an AI-powered solution, specifically an Investment Scouting & Diligence Engine, by translating a solution brief into a realistic implementation plan, thinking clearly about architecture and delivery risk, and communicating effectively with both technical and executive stakeholders. A strong hire for this role must demonstrate technical fluency, prioritization, and the ability to move from concept to a credible implementation approach quickly. They must also show hands-on technical execution and the ability to build a rough working prototype to demonstrate technical capability.

## What each category means here
* **Problem understanding**: The candidate demonstrates a clear grasp of the Investment Scouting & Diligence Engine's objectives, key benefits, and constraints, and engages with the real constraints of the problem rather than a generic version of it.
* **Depth of reasoning**: The candidate provides analysis and supporting evidence for their implementation plan, architecture decisions, and prototype design, weighing trade-offs and alternatives, and naming potential risks and dependencies.
* **Domain craft**: The candidate demonstrates specific skills in AI delivery leadership, such as defining a clear solution architecture, identifying key dependencies and risks, and designing a realistic delivery plan, including phases, launch readiness criteria, and client/executive status updates.
* **Practical execution**: The candidate provides a concrete and actionable implementation plan, including a rough working prototype that demonstrates technical capability, and explains their technical choices and prioritization decisions.
* **Communication**: The candidate communicates clearly, concisely, and credibly, both in writing and in the walkthrough video, to both technical and non-technical stakeholders, including executive/client status updates and AI workflow notes.

## What a strong answer does
* Provides a clear and concise solution architecture that includes data sources, ingestion layer, analysis/synthesis layer, storage or memory layer, workflow orchestration, output interface or reporting layer, and human review or QA points.
* Breaks the project into realistic phases, including discovery and requirements alignment, data/source validation, prototype/MVP build, internal QA, pilot launch, and iteration and scale-up.
* Identifies key dependencies and risks, such as public data quality inconsistency, rate limits or source instability, poor signal quality in news or litigation detection, and unclear success criteria.
* Defines launch readiness criteria, including technical readiness, output quality thresholds, review process, stakeholder signoff, and operational support assumptions.
* Provides a rough working prototype that aggregates public data for a stock or public company and produces a lightweight diligence-style output, including company snapshot, recent market performance, relevant news summary, notable risk factors, and synthesized investment or diligence notes.
* Explains technical choices and prioritization decisions in the walkthrough video, including how AI tools were used to accelerate delivery.
* Communicates clearly and credibly with executive/client status updates and AI workflow notes.

## What a weak answer does
* Fails to provide a clear and concise solution architecture, or omits key components.
* Breaks the project into unrealistic or overly simplistic phases, or fails to identify key dependencies and risks.
* Provides a prototype that does not demonstrate technical capability or is overly simplistic.
* Fails to define launch readiness criteria or provide a clear review process.
* Communicates poorly or unclearly, including vague or generic executive/client status updates and AI workflow notes.
* Fails to explain technical choices and prioritization decisions in the walkthrough video.

## Score bands
* 90-100: The candidate provides a comprehensive and well-structured implementation plan, a clear and concise solution architecture, and a rough working prototype that demonstrates technical capability. They communicate clearly and credibly, both in writing and in the walkthrough video.
* 75-89: The candidate provides a good implementation plan and solution architecture, but may lack clarity or concision in some areas. The prototype demonstrates some technical capability, but may have limitations or areas for improvement. Communication is generally clear, but may have some minor issues.
* 60-74: The candidate provides a fair implementation plan and solution architecture, but may have significant gaps or areas for improvement. The prototype demonstrates some technical capability, but may be overly simplistic or lack key features. Communication is somewhat unclear or vague in some areas.
* 40-59: The candidate provides a poor implementation plan and solution architecture, with significant gaps or areas for improvement. The prototype lacks technical capability or is overly simplistic. Communication is unclear or vague, including executive/client status updates and AI workflow notes.
* 0-39: The candidate fails to provide a credible implementation plan, solution architecture, or prototype, or demonstrates a significant lack of technical capability or understanding of the problem domain. Communication is poor or non-existent.
