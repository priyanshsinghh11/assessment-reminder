## What this assessment tests
A strong hire for this role must demonstrate the ability to analyze complex business processes, identify areas for improvement, and effectively communicate their findings. They must also show a solid understanding of process documentation, automation, and AI opportunities. The ability to learn unfamiliar operations quickly, attention to detail, and critical thinking are essential skills for this role.

## What each category means here
* **Problem understanding**: The candidate's ability to comprehend the current state of the client's process, including its strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement, as evidenced by their analysis of the discovery transcript.
* **Depth of reasoning**: The candidate's ability to think critically and provide well-supported recommendations for process improvement, including their analysis of operational challenges and identification of automation and AI opportunities.
* **Domain craft**: The candidate's demonstration of specific skills in business process analysis, documentation, and improvement, including their ability to create a clear and concise process map, identify information gaps, and develop effective follow-up questions.
* **Practical execution**: The candidate's ability to provide concrete and actionable recommendations for process improvement, including their identification of areas for automation and AI opportunities, and their development of a structured process document.
* **Communication**: The candidate's ability to clearly and effectively communicate their findings, analysis, and recommendations, including their use of clear and concise language, proper formatting, and visual aids such as workflow diagrams.

## What a strong answer does
* Provides a clear and concise process summary, including purpose, stakeholders, systems, and major stages.
* Identifies significant information gaps and unclear responsibilities, and develops effective follow-up questions to address these gaps.
* Creates a clear and well-organized workflow diagram that accurately represents the current process.
* Develops a comprehensive and well-structured process document, including workflow steps, decision points, and known exceptions.
* Identifies significant operational challenges and provides well-supported recommendations for process improvement, including automation and AI opportunities.
* Prioritizes recommendations based on potential impact and feasibility.
* Clearly separates facts from assumptions and provides evidence to support their analysis and recommendations.

## What a weak answer does
* Fails to provide a clear and concise process summary, or omits key stakeholders, systems, or stages.
* Does not identify significant information gaps or unclear responsibilities, or develops ineffective follow-up questions.
* Creates a confusing or inaccurate workflow diagram, or fails to provide a diagram at all.
* Develops a disorganized or incomplete process document, or fails to include key elements such as workflow steps or decision points.
* Identifies few or no operational challenges, or provides unsupported or unrealistic recommendations for process improvement.
* Fails to prioritize recommendations or provide evidence to support their analysis and recommendations.

## Score bands
* 90-100: The candidate provides a comprehensive and well-supported analysis, including a clear and concise process summary, effective follow-up questions, and well-supported recommendations for process improvement. The workflow diagram and process document are clear, well-organized, and accurate.
* 75-89: The candidate provides a good analysis, but may lack clarity or concision in their process summary, or may not fully address all information gaps. The workflow diagram and process document are generally well-organized, but may contain some errors or omissions.
* 60-74: The candidate provides a fair analysis, but may lack depth or insight in their process summary, or may not effectively identify information gaps. The workflow diagram and process document may be disorganized or contain significant errors.
* 40-59: The candidate provides a poor analysis, including a unclear or incomplete process summary, ineffective follow-up questions, and unsupported recommendations for process improvement. The workflow diagram and process document may be missing or of very poor quality.
* 0-39: The candidate fails to provide a meaningful analysis, including a missing or incomplete process summary, no follow-up questions, and no recommendations for process improvement. The workflow diagram and process document are missing or of very poor quality.
