## What this assessment tests
This assessment evaluates a candidate's ability to design and develop a lightweight collaborative document editor, demonstrating their product judgment, full stack capability, and clear prioritization. A strong hire for this role must show sound engineering skills, practical AI usage, and effective communication. They should be able to turn an open-ended prompt into a coherent product slice, execute across frontend, backend, persistence, and access logic, and deliver a high-quality document editing experience.

## What each category means here
* **Problem understanding**: The candidate demonstrates a clear grasp of the assignment's objectives, constraints, and requirements, such as understanding the need for a lightweight collaborative document editor, the importance of prioritization, and the role of AI tools.
* **Depth of reasoning**: The candidate provides thoughtful analysis and trade-off discussions, such as explaining the reasoning behind their technical choices, the limitations of their implementation, and the potential implications of their design decisions.
* **Domain craft**: The candidate showcases specific skills relevant to the AI-Native Full Stack Developer role, such as proficiency in full stack development, experience with collaborative document editing, and knowledge of AI-powered tools and workflows.
* **Practical execution**: The candidate delivers a working, testable, and well-documented implementation, including a clear setup and run instructions, a live deployment, and basic validation and error handling.
* **Communication**: The candidate presents their work in a clear, concise, and structured manner, including a well-written README, a short architecture note, and a walkthrough video that effectively explains their design decisions and implementation choices.

## What a strong answer does
* Provides a clear and concise overview of the project's objectives and scope
* Demonstrates a working, testable, and well-documented implementation of the collaborative document editor
* Includes a well-structured and easy-to-follow README with setup and run instructions
* Shows a clear understanding of the role of AI tools in the development process and provides examples of their practical application
* Presents a live deployment of the application that is easy to access and test
* Includes basic validation and error handling to ensure a robust user experience
* Provides a short architecture note that explains the technical choices and trade-offs made during the development process
* Offers a clear and concise walkthrough video that explains the implementation and design decisions

## What a weak answer does
* Fails to provide a clear overview of the project's objectives and scope
* Delivers a non-working, incomplete, or poorly documented implementation
* Lacks a clear understanding of the role of AI tools in the development process or fails to provide examples of their practical application
* Presents a deployment that is difficult to access or test
* Neglects to include basic validation and error handling, resulting in a poor user experience
* Fails to provide a clear and concise walkthrough video or architecture note
* Demonstrates a lack of prioritization and trade-off awareness, resulting in an overly ambitious or poorly focused implementation

## Score bands
* 90-100: The candidate delivers a comprehensive, well-documented, and fully functional implementation that demonstrates a deep understanding of the assignment's objectives and requirements.
* 75-89: The candidate provides a good implementation that meets most of the requirements, but may lack some features or have some minor issues with documentation or testing.
* 60-74: The candidate delivers a fair implementation that meets some of the requirements, but may have significant issues with functionality, documentation, or testing.
* 40-59: The candidate provides a poor implementation that fails to meet most of the requirements, with significant issues with functionality, documentation, or testing.
* 0-39: The candidate fails to deliver a working implementation or provides a submission that is incomplete, poorly documented, or demonstrates a lack of understanding of the assignment's objectives and requirements.
