## What this assessment tests
This assessment evaluates a candidate's ability to design and deliver a practical AI training session, demonstrating their capacity to lead and deliver AI training in a real-world environment. A strong hire for this role must show the ability to translate AI capabilities into real workflows, communicate clearly, and demonstrate AI tools in action. They must also be able to engage an audience with structured, outcome-driven delivery and operate with strong judgment, clarity, and presence.

## What each category means here
* **Problem understanding**: The candidate demonstrates an understanding of the specific challenges and constraints of training a healthcare organization with limited AI experience, and designs a training session that addresses these needs.
* **Depth of reasoning**: The candidate provides a clear and well-supported rationale for their training design, including the selection of AI workflows and tools, and demonstrates an understanding of the trade-offs and alternatives.
* **Domain craft**: The candidate shows expertise in designing and delivering AI training sessions, particularly in a healthcare context, and demonstrates a strong understanding of how to apply AI tools to real-world workflows.
* **Practical execution**: The candidate provides a concrete and actionable training design, including a clear session structure, practical AI workflows, and step-by-step instructions for participants.
* **Communication**: The candidate communicates their training design and delivery clearly and concisely, both in writing and in the video presentation, and demonstrates the ability to simplify complex concepts and engage an audience.

## What a strong answer does
* Clearly defines the objective and target audience for the training session
* Provides a well-structured and focused training design that addresses the needs of the healthcare organization
* Selects a practical and relevant AI workflow that can be applied in a real-world context
* Demonstrates a clear understanding of how to use AI tools to support the workflow
* Provides a clear and concise presentation deck that supports the training delivery
* Delivers a confident and structured video presentation that demonstrates the AI workflow and engages the audience
* Shows evidence of previous training experience and delivery capability
* Effectively uses AI tools to support the training and demonstrates an understanding of how to apply them in a real-world context

## What a weak answer does
* Fails to clearly define the objective and target audience for the training session
* Provides a training design that is too theoretical or generic, and does not address the specific needs of the healthcare organization
* Selects an AI workflow that is not practical or relevant to the context
* Fails to demonstrate a clear understanding of how to use AI tools to support the workflow
* Provides a presentation deck that is overly complex or confusing
* Delivers a video presentation that is unclear, unstructured, or lacking in confidence
* Fails to provide evidence of previous training experience or delivery capability
* Overemphasizes explanation over demonstration, and fails to show the practical application of AI tools

## Score bands
* 90-100: The candidate provides a comprehensive and well-structured training design, delivers a confident and engaging video presentation, and demonstrates a strong understanding of how to apply AI tools in a real-world context.
* 75-89: The candidate provides a good training design, delivers a clear and structured video presentation, and demonstrates a good understanding of how to apply AI tools, but may lack some depth or clarity in their response.
* 60-74: The candidate provides a fair training design, delivers a video presentation that is somewhat unclear or unstructured, and demonstrates some understanding of how to apply AI tools, but may lack practical experience or delivery capability.
* 40-59: The candidate provides a poor training design, delivers a video presentation that is unclear or unengaging, and demonstrates limited understanding of how to apply AI tools, and may lack evidence of previous training experience or delivery capability.
* 0-39: The candidate fails to provide a coherent training design, delivers a video presentation that is very unclear or unengaging, and demonstrates little or no understanding of how to apply AI tools, and lacks evidence of previous training experience or delivery capability.
